"Download Excel bed plan" fills in your actual CTC Cherry Tree map sheets. If you upload a nominal roll, names are given beds automatically in roll order (so squads stay together), listed under each map and on a new "Bed Allocation" sheet. Every bed number is coloured by who sleeps there, each room's label is updated, and a key is added under each map. Each block prints on one page. If the room layout changes, choose an updated copy of the bed plan under "Bed plan template" – it must keep the same sheets and bed-number cells.

Rules applied: Block 4 is CFAV only and Room 1 is always the Event IC. Every room is single-sex and cadets never share a room with adults. Each of Blocks 1–3 is used by one sex only, because they share ablutions. CFAV are never placed in cadet blocks. Any CFAV who don't fit in Block 4 go into a spare block that has no cadets in it. That block can hold male and female CFAV, but each room is single-sex. Always check the plan against your safeguarding requirements before issue.
